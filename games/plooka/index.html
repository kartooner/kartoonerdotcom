Plooka!

Bounce. Pop. Dig deeper.

A cozy, hand-drawn game where you bounce balls off numbered blocks and dig as deep as you can.

Coming soon to iPhone and iPad

Play the free web version

The web version is called Boop!, after the little blue block.

How it plays

Every block has a number. Each hit knocks it down by one, and at zero it pops. Clear the board and you drop to the next one. There's no bottom, so the only question is how far you'll get.

Touch where you want to aim and a dotted line shows every bounce before you shoot. Then knock out the nails and watch blocks swing and fall, set off a bomb, snap a vine. The deeper you dig, the more little surprises turn up.

Boop, the little blue block, keeps you company and points out new things as you meet them.

Made to feel good

  • No ads. Nothing to buy. Pay once and it's yours.
  • Relaxed mode for little ones or slow evenings: easier zones, shots that never run out.
  • Saves itself, so you can put it down any time.
  • Par for every zone, if you like a challenge.
  • Easy on the eyes, with dark mode and Reduce Motion support.

Made by Erik Sagen, who mostly wanted to see if physics could feel cozy.
