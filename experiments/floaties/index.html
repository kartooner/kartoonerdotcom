Primordial Glow

Nebula Floaties

How to play

You're a tiny glowing snake in the deep. Eat, grow, and rise up through the layers of the nebula. One life per run: see how high you can get.

Other creatures

Their head colour tells you how they compare to you, and it changes as you grow.

Green
Smaller than you. Swim into any part of them to eat them. They sprint away, then tire out: a pale head means now's your chance.
Red
Bigger than you. They chase you for a while. Meet one head on and it bites a chunk of your mass.
Grey
About your size. They mind their own business.
Violet tail
A rival snake. Rivals eat bits and grow like you do, and when they're bigger they try to cut across your path.

Bodies are walls

Swim your head into the body of anything that isn't green and you crash. On layers 1 and 2 a crash just costs you mass, like a bite. From layer 3 on, a crash ends the run.

It works the other way too: anything that hits your body crashes and bursts into remains. Curl around a big red creature and make it run into you.

Layers and mutations

Reach 50 mass to rise to the next layer. The camera pulls back, the layer you were on sinks below you, and a new layer full of bigger things begins. Then pick one of three mutations. They last for the rest of the run, and some stack.

Runs and unlocks

The first two layers go easy on you while you learn. After that, hunters get more common, faster and hungrier, bites take more, and the harsher conditions show up.
