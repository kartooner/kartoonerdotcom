Design philosophy

I focus on clarity, people, and results. Using empathy, systems thinking, and creative problem-solving, I design solutions that work for users and work for the business.

I lead with intent first, not outputs. AI is part of that process too, as a design partner for sharper thinking, not a replacement for it.

So I wrote it down. Viking is my AI-intent-first design framework; it's open source on GitHub.
